Phase 1 — Engagement

ALL TYPES
From enquiry to booked meeting · ~1.5 hr work · 1–2 weeks elapsed
  1. SETUP- Acknowledge enquiry, email, name sales task
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee
    Sequencer entry point. Salesperson replies to the inbound enquiry within ~1 business hour, confirms receipt, and renames the parent deal task with the convention TS{YYMMDD} {Surname} — {Project type}. Closing this subtask fires Make scenario 5490913, which auto-creates Project information requested. The first response sets the responsiveness clock for the whole deal.
  2. Project information requested
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 Auto-spawned
    Email the client requesting whatever scope detail is missing — plans, photos, brief, site address, intended use, budget range. The salesperson sends and waits. Closing this subtask without the client replying triggers Follow up #1; closing it after the client supplies the info triggers Make Online Meeting Booking.
  3. Follow up #1- Project information (spawned only if no reply within 3d)
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +3d after request
    Auto-cancels if a sibling subtask Project information supplied is closed in the meantime (sequencer rule #5). Otherwise, polite chase email — keep it short, restate what's missing, suggest a 15-min discovery call as an alternative.
  4. Follow up #2- Project information (spawned only if still no reply)
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +3d after #1
    Final chase. Closing this advances to Make Online Meeting Booking regardless of whether the client supplied info — at this point we book a meeting to extract it verbally rather than chase forever via email.
  5. Make Online Meeting Booking
    ⏱ 30 min 📅 Due in 2d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee
    Send Calendly / direct booking link to the client. Includes the meeting itself (~30 min discovery) and any pre-meeting prep. Closing this subtask fires the type-specific scoping & pricing chain (Phase 2 + Phase 3).

Phase 2 — Discovery & Scoping

TYPE-SPECIFIC
Site visit + scope-of-work definition · 2–6 hr work · 5 days elapsed
  1. Site measure
    ⏱ 2 hr 📅 Due in 5d 👷 Field team / parent owner
    Physical site visit to measure, photograph, and assess. Output is a measure file uploaded to the deal. Skipped for pure Cert deals where the certifier can work off existing plans — confirm with you.
  2. Design fee scope
    ⏱ 1 hr 📅 Due in 3d 🎨 Design team
    Define the design deliverable: revisions included, drawing types, certifier coordination, council liaison, etc. Translate into a fixed design fee or hourly estimate.
  3. Cert scope (identify required certs + quote)
    ⏱ 2 hr 📅 Due in 3d 📋 Cert specialist
    Identify which certifications apply (BCA report, fire safety, structural sign-off, acoustic, accessibility) and gather quotes from external consultants. Output: a line-item cert quote that rolls into the consolidated proposal.

Phase 3 — Pricing

TYPE-SPECIFIC
Plans, models, and dollar figures · 3–14 hr work · 5 days elapsed
  1. Plans for Quoting (Rayon)
    ⏱ 4 hr 📅 Due in 5d 📐 CAD team
    Rayon is SBI's 2D drawing program. Always required for Build deals — every build needs a Rayon plan before Buildsoft can estimate. Architectural plans (whether SBI-produced via the design team or client-supplied) feed in here as inputs; Rayon translates them into the build drawing set used for pricing and site. Closing this stage is one of the two gates that fire Buildsoft Pricing.
  2. Skp for Quoting
    ⏱ 3 hr 📅 Due in 5d 🪚 Joinery team
    SketchUp model of the joinery — cabinet boxes, doors, hardware, finishes. Drives Mozaik pricing and becomes part of the client presentation. Sibling gate with Plans for Quoting — both must close before the consolidated pricing step fires.
  3. Mozaik — Joinery Plans + Pricing
    ⏱ 3 hr 📅 Due in 3d 🪚 Factory / CAD
    Mozaik does the joinery plans (shop drawings — sections, elevations, hardware schedules) and the CNC file processing for manufacturing. SketchUp output from the previous stage feeds in for the visual model; Mozaik produces the production-grade joinery plans, the cut list/materials/hardware, the price, and the CNC files. Note for sequencer rev: the current playbook stage name "Price up Build, Joinery in Mozaik" understates the scope — Mozaik is a plans tool too, not just pricing.
  4. Buildsoft Pricing
    ⏱ 4 hr 📅 Due in 5d 💰 Estimator
    Buildsoft estimate from approved plans — labour, materials, subbie quotes, contingency. Output exported to Excel into 1.2 COST SHEETS/ for the proposal. Sub-quotes from the Contractors & Suppliers list are gathered here via the SBI-RFQ-Contractors-Suppliers skill.

Phase 4 — Presentation

ALL TYPES
Qwilr proposal + the live presentation · 3 hr work · 1 week elapsed
  1. Online Presentation Ready (Qwilr / Plans / Skp)
    ⏱ 2 hr 📅 Due in 3d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee
    Assemble the Qwilr proposal page — headline, value props, plans/Skp embeds, fixed price, terms. Triggered via the qwilr-proposals skill which generates the HTML structure. Closing this signals "ready to present" — typically followed by booking the presentation slot.
  2. Online Presentation- Done
    ⏱ 1 hr 📅 Due in 7d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee
    The actual Qwilr walkthrough call — present, answer questions, take feedback. Closing this kicks the deal into the closing-follow-ups stream. Update the parent deal status to PRESENTATION SENT at this point.

Phase 5 — Closing Follow-ups

ALL TYPES
Five-step nudge sequence · ~1 hr work · 5 weeks elapsed
  1. Follow-up #1
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +2d after presentation
    Permissive stream — sequencer can skip ahead or hold off if it sees recent client activity. The aim is steady contact without pestering.
  2. Follow-up #2
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +4d after #1
    Different angle from #1 — lead with new info or social proof (recent similar project, photo).
  3. Follow-up #3
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +7d after #2
    Mid-cycle — try to surface objections directly. "What's holding you back from saying yes?" type message.
  4. Follow-up #4
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +10d after #3
    Last serious nudge before the closing email. Often the most effective — clients have had time to compare, may now be ready to commit.
  5. Follow-up #5 Send Closing email
    ⏱ 15 min 📅 Due in 1d 👤 SBI Workflow Assignee 🔄 +14d after #4
    "Closing the loop — let me know if circumstances change" — politely puts the deal to bed without burning the relationship. Closing this subtask ends the chain (sequencer rule: Follow-up #5 → do_nothing).
