How a Sale Runs — Enquiry → Proposal → Won

Architecture v2 (2026-05-30) · ClickUp Deals list + template task 86d2ufymy
The model: every new deal arrives with the full pipeline pre-populated (~29 subtasks, owners stamped, no due dates). The team works the visible structure; the AI Sales Director on the dashboard advises which task to activate next. Dates are decisions humans make — not assumptions baked in. (The old auto-sequencer is retired.)

⭐ Gail runs the chase engine — three jobs

  1. Open & qualify (P1) — acknowledge, request info, send booking link. The SETUP 6-item contact gate (name · email · phone · address · ack sent · deal renamed) must be ticked before SETUP can close.
  2. Drive every follow-up — P1 info chases, P4 booking chases + decline, P5 closing chases. Nothing goes quiet.
  3. Record the outcome — won/lost; the win fires the project handover.
