How a Sale Runs — Enquiry → Proposal → Won

Process v0.12 (updated 7 June 2026) · ClickUp Deals list + template task 86d2ufymy
The model: every new deal arrives with the full pipeline pre-populated (~29 subtasks, owners stamped in the 🤝 SBI Workflow Assignee field, no due dates). The team works the visible structure; the AI Sales Director on the dashboard advises which task to activate next, and the 📊 Deal Snapshot on each deal says where it's up to. A deal's stage = its ClickUp status. Only Michael & Gail move a deal between stages. (The old auto-sequencer is retired.)

⭐ Gail runs the chase engine — three jobs

  1. Open & qualify (P1) — acknowledge, request info, send booking link. The SETUP 6-item contact gate (name · email · phone · address · ack sent · deal renamed) must be ticked before SETUP can close.
  2. Drive every follow-up — P1 info chases (~4 → polite close), P4 booking chases (up to 5 → politely decline), P5 closing chases (up to 5). Nothing goes quiet.
  3. Record the outcome — won / lost; the win fires the project handover.
The pipeline · P1 → P6
📞 P1 · Engagement

Open & qualify the deal

Acknowledge → request project info → send the meeting booking link, chasing as needed. Qualify out wrong-type work (handyman, out of region, outside ~$5k–$750k).

Gail inquiries recieved → active outreach → qualified lead
  • SETUP + 6-item contact gate
  • Project-info request + ~3 chases + polite close
  • Intro-meeting booking link
→
🔍 P2 · Discovery

Scope the job

Online intro meeting (the default) or a site inspection to understand the work. Skip straight to pricing if we already have enough to quote (usually joinery).

Michael initial meeting booked → meet/inspection completed
  • Hold intro meeting / site measure
  • "meet/inspection completed" = a quick holding step on the way to pricing
→
💰 P3 · Pricing

Price it (parallel tracks)

Plans + costs built in parallel, then consolidated into a proposal. Job type picks the tracks: joinery-only → Mozaik; on-site build → Buildsoft; combined → both.

MichaelShravanChadd estimating & design
  • Plans + SketchUp + site markup — Michael
  • Mozaik joinery pricing — Shravan
  • Buildsoft build pricing — Chadd
  • Build deal awaiting our own design parks here — not stalled.
🎯 P4 · Presentation

Get in front of the client

Gail sends the booking link & chases it. Michael presents live in the meeting — the Qwilr goes out after (sending without the meeting cuts the win rate).

GailMichael presentation meeting
  • Send booking link + chase up to 5 → politely decline — Gail
  • Present the proposal live — Michael
→
🤝 P5 · Closing

Follow up to a yes

Escalating follow-ups for the decision: +2 / +4 / +7 / +10 / +14 days (#5 = closing email). On a verbal yes, chase the signature.

Gail presentation sent → contracts requested
  • Closing follow-ups #1–#5
  • Verbal yes → contracts requested → chase the Qwilr signature
→
💰 P6 · Reprice

Refine & re-present

↩ Near-close loop — a deal here is nearly sold, not stalled.

Client wants changes after seeing the proposal. Michael reprices off the existing inputs, then it goes back in front of them. (A small tweak — a finish, a date — just goes back as an updated Qwilr; no reprice.)

MichaelGail reprice proposal → represent proposal → (back to contracts)
  • Reprice — Michael · ~2–3 biz days
  • Re-present revised proposal — Gail / Michael
  • More than 2 reprices = scope churn, flagged.
✅ WON — client signs the Qwilr → closed won → becomes a project (deposit invoice + official contracts go out)
How a deal ends — holding states & the two ways it's lost

⏸ Holding (still live)

  • on hold drip feed — engaged, just needs time. Re-contact at least monthly; re-engages → back to the exact stage it was parked from.
  • client ghosting — gone silent during/after a chase. Holds ~3 weeks, then a terminal below.

⛔ Lost terminals

  • done lost after proposal — we priced it / built a proposal (P3 on) but didn't win.
  • declined_no proposal — fell out before any pricing (P1 chases exhausted, wrong type, never qualified).
🧭 The one rule: when a deal is lost, ask "did we do the pricing?" — Yes → done lost after proposal · No → declined_no proposal.
Where things are saved: documents live in the job's Shared Drive folder and are linked from the deal in ClickUp. Paste each link the moment the document is produced (Buildsoft link at P3, proposal at P4) — so ClickUp is always the live index, not a tidy-up at the end.
