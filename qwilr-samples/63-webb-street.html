Three quiet differences

Three details that quietly separate a considered build from a quick one.

01

11 Lm of Easy Baffle laid through the ceiling cavity

Most builders treat sound as a wall problem, full stop. The reality is that conversations and TV bridge from one side of the wall to the other over the top of it — through the open cavity above the ceiling. That's the path the Easy Baffle closes. Without it, you'd hear next door even with a perfectly built partition below.

02

50mm polyester acoustic batts inside the new partition

The cavity inside a 64mm steel stud is the cheapest acoustic upgrade in the build. Polyester batts aren't there for thermal — they're there for privacy between rooms. A budget builder leaves the cavity empty and tells you it "meets code". You can hear the difference the day you walk through.

03

25mm clear anodised flat bar where the wall meets the ceiling

Where new gyprock meets the existing ceiling, most builders leave a raw plaster line. We finish it with a 25mm anodised aluminium flat bar — a small detail that turns a builder's wall into an architectural one. Clients won't tell you why it looks better. They'll feel it the moment they walk in.
