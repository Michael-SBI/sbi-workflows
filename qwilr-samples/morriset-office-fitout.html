Where Glass-Walled Boardrooms Fail

Glass partitions are the #1 acoustic failure point in mezzanine offices. Most builders spec 10mm float glass with brush-strip seals — looks identical to a premium spec, but every conversation in the boardroom carries to the workstations downstairs. SBI specs acoustic-rated laminated glazing with full-perimeter intumescent seals (head, jamb, threshold) — delivering Rw35-38 in glass and Rw45 in the solid framed walls behind it. The difference between a boardroom that feels confidential and one that doesn't is roughly $40 a linear metre, made invisible at handover.
