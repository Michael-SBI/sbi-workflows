The Project Vision

Three small but defining changes to the Mann Street office. A refreshed kitchen with proper overhead, table and tall storage joinery; a new secure storeroom built into the existing layout with full-height acoustic partitioning and two fire-rated doors; and the finishing detail — paint, hardware and trims — that lifts the whole space from "office" to "client-ready".

Delivered as a single coordinated program by an SBI Project Manager. One face, one schedule, one handover — minimal disruption to client meetings during the works.

Technical Specs

  • New partition: 5 Lm × 2700H, 64mm steel stud, 13mm plasterboard both sides, 50mm polyester acoustic batts, 25mm clear anodised flat bar at top
  • Doors: 2 × solid MDF 2700×900×35mm with fire-rated lever entrance sets, door stops, timber jambs
  • Joinery (Mozaik): J01 overhead with microwave cabinet · J02 kitchen table · J03 tall kitchen cabinet
  • Painting: New walls, new doors and trims — colour TBC at pre-start
  • Demolition: Existing overhead cupboards removed and disposed
  • Hardware: Padlock and hasp added to existing sliding door

Three details that quietly separate commercial-grade from off-the-shelf

1
50mm acoustic batts in the new partition wall

The polyester insulation in the storeroom partition isn't there for thermal — it's there for client confidentiality. In a conveyancing fitout, conversations carry. We spec the batts so a client meeting on one side of the wall doesn't reach the storeroom or kitchen on the other. A budget builder would leave the cavity empty and tell you it "meets code".

2
25mm clear anodised flat bar at the top of the wall

Where the new gyprock meets the existing ceiling, most builders leave a raw plaster line. We finish it with a 25mm clear anodised aluminium flat bar — a $40-a-metre detail that turns a builder's wall into an architectural one. Clients won't tell you why it looks better, but they'll feel it the moment they walk in.

3
Fire-rated lever entrance sets on commercial doors

Solid MDF doors with fire-rated lever sets are commercial-grade hardware — engineered for daily commercial use over 10+ years. Most off-the-shelf joinery suppliers would substitute a residential lever set to keep the price down, and you'd be replacing handles in 18 months. We use the commercial spec from day one.
