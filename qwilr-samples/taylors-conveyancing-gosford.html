Three details that quietly separate commercial-grade from off-the-shelf.

01

50mm acoustic batts in the partition wall

Polyester insulation isn't there for thermal — it's there for client confidentiality. Conversations carry in conveyancing offices. The batts mean a meeting on one side of the wall doesn't reach the storerooms or kitchen on the other. A budget builder leaves the cavity empty and tells you it "meets code".

02

25mm clear anodised flat bar at the top of the wall

Where new gyprock meets existing ceiling, most builders leave a raw plaster line. We finish it with a 25mm anodised aluminium flat bar — a $40-a-metre detail that turns a builder's wall into an architectural one. Clients won't tell you why it looks better. They'll feel it the moment they walk in.

03

Fire-rated lever entrance sets on commercial doors

Solid MDF doors with fire-rated commercial lever sets are engineered for daily commercial use over 10+ years. Off-the-shelf joinery suppliers substitute residential levers to keep the price down — and you replace handles in 18 months. Commercial spec, day one.
